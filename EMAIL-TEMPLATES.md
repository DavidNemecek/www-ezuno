# Aktuelle E-Mail-Vorlagen

Nur die sieben ehemaligen Formularstellen verwenden diese drei Vorlagen.
Empfänger ist immer `ezuno@ezuno.com`. Die Texte sind Englisch wie die Website.
Besucher ergänzen und versenden die Nachricht in ihrem eigenen Mailprogramm.

Quelle: [`tools/static_forms.py`](tools/static_forms.py). Historische Felder:
[`FORM-MAPPING.md`](FORM-MAPPING.md).

## Request free license

**Betreff:** ezuno - Free BASIC license request

```text
Hello ezuno team,

I would like to request a free BASIC license for up to 10 users.

Name: [Your name]
Company / organization (optional): [Your company]
Email address for the license: [Your email address]

Thank you.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
```

## Contact sales

**Betreff:** ezuno - Sales and pricing enquiry

```text
Hello ezuno team,

I am interested in ezuno and would like to discuss the following request.

Request: [Quote, purchase, evaluation or reseller enquiry]
Number of users: [Required number of users]
Current evaluation (optional): [Not started / FREE BASIC / other]
Company / organization (optional): [Your company]

My questions or requirements:
[Please describe your request here.]

Kind regards,
[Your name]
```
